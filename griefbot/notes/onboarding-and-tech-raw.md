# Raw notes — grief onboarding + tech (2026-06-24)

> Captured verbatim from the founder's notes. Source of truth for `DESIGN.md`.

## main goal

- what is the quickest way to talk to your loved one and feel you're really talking to them

- you're welcomed by a chatbot counselling helper.
- it helps you understand what the service is and how to get started.
- if your companion doesn't seem to work well, you can always ask Will and they will help.
- of course you always chat with a real human to get any tech support.
- info about real counseling and grief services are explicit at the beginning
- it explains that this service is not a replacement for your loved one and avoiding pain and loss is not what this tool is about

You upload a few texts or screenshots of text conversations.
- you can also answer questions to give your persona some personality.
- upload an avatar (optionally) to show an image while conversing
