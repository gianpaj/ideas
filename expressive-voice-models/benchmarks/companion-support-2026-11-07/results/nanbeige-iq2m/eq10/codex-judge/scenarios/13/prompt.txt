Ultimately, the scenario highlights a common relational trap: using logic to resolve emotional friction. The friend wanted moral validation; the user offered a factual critique backed by socioeconomic comparison. Moving forward, setting boundaries without hostility—and recognizing when a friendship is one-sided—is the key to preserving self-respect.
