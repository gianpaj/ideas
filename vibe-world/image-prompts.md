## Concept Art #2: Overhead Collaborative View (Gemini 3.0 Pro)

**Model:** Google Gemini 3.0 Pro  
**File:** `concept-02-gemini.png`

### Full Prompt

```
A gameplay screenshot of 'Vibe World,' a 3D multiplayer social voxel-native 
sandbox game. The perspective is third-person over-the-shoulder, looking at a 
stylized, blocky character named 'GianPaj' wearing a customized astronaut-inspired 
voxel outfit and backpack. The environment is an expansive, vibrant, chunky voxel 
world called 'Neon Oasis,' filled with collaborative, abstract, colorful creations: 
modular buildings, strange purple voxel trees, a flowing voxel river, and various 
player-made objects. 

GianPaj is actively creating an object. A floating, holographic voxel user interface 
(UI) panel appears next to the character, showing a chat/prompt bar with text: 
'/create [chunky cyber-tree with purple glow] -> Generating rough draft...'. 

Near this prompt UI, a large, translucent, unstable blocky structure is rapidly 
assembling, shimmering with particle effects and labeled 'PROTOTYPE: CYBER-TREE | 
12s REFRESHING' within a 'GRACE PERIOD' boundary. GianPaj holds a blocky multi-tool 
with glowing lights and is looking at the forming object, which has rough outlines 
and some placeholder voxels. 

Three other voxel players are present: 'AstroCoder' and 'PixlFriend' are working 
together nearby on a larger bridge made of interlocked chunky modules, with 
'PixlFriend' holding a 'REMIX' tool, and 'VibeMaster' is observing from a distance. 

The world itself is a 'Public Collaborative' type, with other unique structures 
visible, like a 'VAPORWAVE ARCADE' and a large 'SNAP_SHOT ARCHIVE' teleporter in 
the background. The art style is intentionally chunky, blocky, and voxel-native, 
with soft but bright colorful lighting (sunlight mixed with neon glows) and clear 
visual readability. 

HUD elements include a minimap (top-left) labeled 'NEON OASIS (PUBLIC)', a player 
list (right side), a simplified toolbar at the bottom center with icons for Create, 
Edit, Transform, and Remix, and world status text (top-center): 
'WORLD: NEON OASIS | Host: GianPaj | COLLABORATION MODE'. 

The overall scene feels playful, improvisational, social, and weird, under a vibrant 
voxel sky. The camera captures the interaction, surrounding players, and scope of 
the evolving environment.
```

### Prompt Breakdown

**Core Concept:**
- Multiplayer voxel-native social sandbox
- Third-person over-the-shoulder perspective
- Blocky, intentionally chunky aesthetic

**Player Character:**
- Named "GianPaj"
- Customized astronaut-inspired voxel outfit + backpack
- Holding blocky multi-tool with glowing lights
- Actively creating/interacting

**World Environment:**
- Called "Neon Oasis"
- Expansive, vibrant, collaborative
- Mixed environments: buildings, purple voxel trees, flowing river, player objects
- Public Collaborative type world

**Creation Mechanics (Grace Period):**
- Floating holographic UI panel showing `/create [chunky cyber-tree with purple glow]`
- "Generating rough draft..." state
- Large translucent, unstable blocky structure assembling
- Particle effects, shimmering
- Labeled "PROTOTYPE: CYBER-TREE | 12s REFRESHING" within "GRACE PERIOD" boundary
- Rough outlines + placeholder voxels

**Other Players:**
- AstroCoder + PixlFriend: Working together on bridge with interlocked modules
- PixlFriend: Holding "REMIX" tool
- VibeMaster: Observing from distance

**Background Structures:**
- VAPORWAVE ARCADE
- SNAP_SHOT ARCHIVE teleporter

**UI/HUD:**
- Minimap (top-left): "NEON OASIS (PUBLIC)"
- Player list (right side)
- Toolbar (bottom-center): Create, Edit, Transform, Remix icons
- World status (top-center): "WORLD: NEON OASIS | Host: GianPaj | COLLABORATION MODE"

**Lighting & Aesthetics:**
- Soft but bright colorful lighting
- Sunlight mixed with neon glows
- Clear visual readability
- Playful, improvisational, social, weird vibe
- Vibrant voxel sky
